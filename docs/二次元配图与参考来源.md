## Q 版角色完整提示词

工具设置：`transparent_background: true`。输入参考图为上述主图。

```text
Use the referenced illustration's silver-haired adult anime maker as the identity and color reference. Transform that same fictional character into a charming full-body chibi mascot for a personal electronics blog. Keep her silver-white hair, blue eyes, little blue hair detail, and casual cobalt-blue and white hoodie. Big expressive head, small simplified proportions, fully covered modest zipped hoodie, navy trousers and white sneakers. She smiles warmly, raises one hand in a friendly wave, and holds a tiny generic green circuit board in the other. Clean polished anime linework, soft cel shading, blue-white pastel palette. Show the complete head, waving fingers, body and both shoes within frame with generous padding. A single centered character cutout only, genuinely transparent alpha background, no backdrop, no floor, no checkerboard pattern, no cast shadow, no border, no lettering, no logo or watermark. Designed as a small friendly decorative mascot beside a website GitHub link.
```

以后需要不同发色、服装、表情时，可在上述提示词基础上调整。生成结果每次可能不同；当前交付的原图已经随源码保存。
